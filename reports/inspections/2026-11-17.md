## Root-cause analysis

Nothing to fix in code.

- **`no_slot`** is `apps/backend/src/fleet/claim.ts:475`: no free fleet slot
  for the worker. Claim tickets minted: `worker-60-14-3` 238, `homelab-8`
  236, `homelab-5` 236, about one every 6 minutes each, none claimed. The
  worker exits before opening a browser, so these are not Facebook logins.
- **Fleet `422`s are stalled request bodies, not bad payloads.** Every slow
  non-long-poll request in the window is one of them: two `homelab/report`
  and three `worker/session` uploads took 34–219 s to arrive between 14:46
  and 15:00, and one of each took 62 s and 91 s at 03:06. The 219 s session
  PUT ended at 15:00:56 together with 16 heartbeat / next-task / events
  requests answered in ~60 ms, all `422`. Backend traffic dipped in both
  windows (43–55 req/min against the usual ~85). The handlers parse with
  `c.req.json().catch(() => null)` and answer `422` when the body is missing
  or cut off (`apps/backend/src/fleet/hosts.ts:537-539`,
  `apps/backend/src/fleet/claim.ts:294-295`, `:796-797`, `:849-850`,
  `:894-896`). Normal-sized reports and sessions from the same host succeed
  the rest of the day, so the payloads are valid; the bodies were truncated
  in transit from the homelab, through the Cloudflare Worker, to Railway.
  Workers retried on their next cycle; there were no follow-on errors.
- **`homelab-6` out of automatic resumes.** It parked `DEAD` 9 times; the ERP
  sent 8 automatic resumes on the `FLEET_AUTO_RESUME_MINUTES` schedule
  (`packages/shared/src/fleet.ts:63`, 10, 30, then 60 minutes), and
  `fleetAutoResumeDue` (`fleet.ts:70-71`) returns `null` once the 8 tries are
  spent. The ninth park, 11-16 14:20, has had no resume since, as designed.

## Fixes deferred

- **Fleet capacity exceeds free slots (fifth night running).** Hosts
  `homelab` and `worker-60-14` want more containers than there are free
  accounts. **Decision for the owner:** lower those hosts' capacity by the
  surplus on the Hosts tab (stops containers, no login), or add accounts to
  the pool (those workers would then claim and log in).
- **`homelab-6` parked `DEAD` with auto-resume spent.** It now needs a
  manager. **Decision for the owner:** check the park's `detail` and its
  proxy's health on the worker page; fix or move the proxy before a manual
  resume, since each resume restores the browser session and carries
  checkpoint risk. Leaving it parked costs nothing.
- **Optional: tell a cut-off body from a bad one.** A truncated upload is
  answered `422 bad_request`, the same as an invalid payload, which makes the
  logs read like a contract break. Answering `400` with a `detail` like
  `body unreadable` when `c.req.json()` throws would separate the two.
  **Decision for the owner:** whether that is worth a contract change; the
  worker image and fleet agent would have to treat the new status the same
  way they treat `422` today. Not urgent; nothing was lost.
