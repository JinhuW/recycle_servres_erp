## Root-cause analysis

- **#1:** `apps/backend/src/routes/scan.ts:117-124` passes the uploaded bytes to
  `scanLabel`. The provider rejected them as a truncated image, and the route
  maps every OCR failure to 502, with a message that says "the AI recognition
  service is unavailable". The status is correct (an upstream rejection). The
  wording is misleading when the image itself is bad, but at one occurrence
  this is not worth a change. Revisit only if it recurs.
- **#2–#4:** outside this repo's code (#2 is Railway's Postgres image, #3 was
  a person at a psql prompt), or harmless (#4).

800 container starts a day for nothing. **Decision for the owner:** lower
`homelab` and `worker-60-14` capacity by the number of idle ids on the ERP
