## Root-cause analysis

**#1, the Telegram user session.** This is the same diagnosis as the
2026-11-23 report. `apps/backend/src/lib/telegramUser.ts` turns the MTProto
`RPCError` into `TelegramError('AUTH_KEY_DUPLICATED')`. `startUserSessionLoop`
in `apps/backend/src/services/fbChatTelegram.ts` fails on connect and retries
every 30 minutes (`USER_FAIL_BACKOFF_MS`), which explains the steady ~2 lines
an hour across all three deployments. Telegram revokes an authorization key
for good once two connections use it at the same time, so these retries
can't succeed until the session is replaced.

**#3, the fleet worker 422s.** Each of these requests took between 19 and
113 seconds. A JSON body that small arrives in milliseconds when the link is
healthy, so the worker's connection stalled and dropped mid-upload. The
route maps the parse failure to `422 bad_request`. The requests clustered
within a few minutes on one afternoon and the endpoints recovered on their
own. I treat this as host-network noise.
