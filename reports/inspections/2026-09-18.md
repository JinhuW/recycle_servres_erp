**RS-068 in detail** (ticket `docs/tickets/RS-068-realized-profit-and-the-cost-rule-review-fixes-befor.md` on `dev`, not present on this branch): before promoting the v1.147.1–v1.149.3 batch to `main`, a `high`-effort review of the `origin/main..origin/dev` diff caught and fixed four findings — the two most consequential:

- `lib/po-cost.ts` `poRealizedLateral` (sold-unit cost) and `services/poRealized.ts` (commission netting) both mis-handled negotiated lot prices and unpriced lines in the new manager-only **Realized profit** figure from RS-064 (v1.149.0), so realized profit could read materially high on affected POs. This was fixed and shipped in the same v1.149.4 commit as RS-064 first appeared on `main` — i.e. the buggy version of Realized profit **never independently reached production**, since `main` only advanced to v1.149.4, post-fix.
- `services/orderAdvance.ts`'s no-cost submit guard (RS-063, v1.148.0) fired on *every* Draft → non-Draft move rather than only a PO's first submission, contradicting its own ticket/changelog ("orders already past Draft are untouched"). This one **did** ship live on `main` at v1.148.0 (2026-09-18 01:49 UTC) and stayed live until the RS-068 fix at v1.149.4 (2026-09-18 05:20 UTC) — roughly **3.5 hours**. During that window, any legacy `$0` PO sent back to Draft (a manager's change-review rejection, or per `revertOrderToDraftTx`, nothing more than the carrier poll's next scan) would have been stuck: a manager's approve got a raw 409, and the tracking poll would have logged `missing: cost` on every scan. The ticket notes 43 such legacy `$0` POs exist in production. **This routine cannot confirm from logs whether any of the 43 were actually touched during that 3.5-hour window**, since Railway access was already unavailable when it happened.
