## Root-cause analysis

Nothing to fix in code.

- **`no_slot`** is `apps/backend/src/fleet/claim.ts:475`, returned when no
  free fleet slot exists for the worker. The worker exits 75 before it opens a
  browser (`trackers/facebook/rs_worker/entry.py`), so these retries are not
  Facebook logins. Claim tickets minted: `homelab-5` 239, `homelab-8` 235,
  `worker-60-14-3` 237, about one every 6 minutes each, none claimed.
- **`homelab-6` parking `DEAD`.** Each park released its leased city
  (`claim.ts:629-632`) and the ERP queued an automatic `resume` on the schedule
  in `packages/shared/src/fleet.ts` (`FLEET_AUTO_RESUME_MINUTES` = 10, 30, 60…
  minutes). The resume counter resets after an hour of `HEALTHY`
  (`claim.ts:594-596`), which is why the gaps fell back to 10 minutes at 13:48
  and 18:56. So the code is doing what it was built to do: the worker recovers
  for a while, then parks again. Its proxy or account is flaky.

## Fixes deferred

- **Fleet capacity exceeds free slots (fourth night running).** Hosts
  `homelab` and `worker-60-14` want more containers than there are free
  accounts. **Decision for the owner:** lower those hosts' capacity by the
  surplus on the Hosts tab, or add accounts to the pool. Lowering capacity
  stops containers and causes no login; adding accounts would make these
  workers claim and log in.
- **`homelab-6` cycles DEAD → resume (new).** 16 parks and 15 auto-resumes in
  24 hours, last park 11-16 00:39. Each resume restarts its browser session,
  which carries checkpoint risk. **Decision for the owner:** look at the
  park's `detail` on the worker page and at its proxy's health; if the proxy
  is the cause, move the slot to another proxy, or park the worker until it is
  fixed so the auto-resumes stop.
