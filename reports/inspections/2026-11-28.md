## Root-cause analysis

**1. Worker session save over 1 MiB (RS-619).**

- In `apps/backend/src/index.ts:324-337` (on `dev`), every route that isn't
  on a special list gets the 1 MiB `jsonBodyLimit`. That includes
  `PUT /api/fleet/worker/session` (`src/fleet/claim.ts:892`).
- Since facebook-tracker 0.14.8 (RS-419), the worker uploads its full
  Playwright `storage_state(indexed_db=True)`
  (`trackers/facebook/rs_poster/rs_poster/session.py:250`). Facebook's
  IndexedDB grows as a session ages.
- The cap answers 413 before `workerAuth` runs, so the log line names no
  worker or account. The 30-minute cadence fits a single worker's save loop.
- Other workers' saves went through as usual. In the last hour, for example,
  accounts 1, 9, 11, 12, 14, 129 and 163 logged `fleet session resealed`.

**2. Telegram `AUTH_KEY_DUPLICATED`.**

- Telegram returns this error when the same auth key is used from two
  connections at once, and then revokes the key.
- `services/fbChatTelegram.ts:77` reads `FB_CHAT_TELEGRAM_SESSION` and
  retries every 1800 s, as designed.
- The only fix is a new session string. This env var is also a candidate to
  move into the database under the repo's secrets rule.
