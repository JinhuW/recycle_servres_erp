## Root-cause analysis

**#1, the Telegram user session.** This is the same diagnosis as the
2026-11-23 and 2026-11-24 reports. `apps/backend/src/lib/telegramUser.ts`
turns the MTProto `RPCError` into `TelegramError('AUTH_KEY_DUPLICATED')`.
`startUserSessionLoop` (`apps/backend/src/services/fbChatTelegram.ts:862`)
holds a Postgres advisory lock before connecting, so the old and new
instances of one deploy never share the session. The lock only covers one
database, though, so a second ERP or a local process holding the same
`FB_CHAT_TELEGRAM_SESSION` is not excluded. That fits what Telegram returns
when two connections use one authorization key at once. Telegram then
revokes the key for good. The loop backs off through `USER_FAIL_BACKOFF_MS`
(`:66`, 1 min → 5 min → 30 min) and retries every 30 minutes, which gives
the steady ~2 lines an hour. Every new deploy resets the backoff. The retries
can't succeed until the session is replaced.

**#2, claim `no_slot`.** `claim.ts:466` answers 409 when the host has no free
slot. A worker re-tickets every ~6 minutes. Behaviour is correct. Whether
that worker should exist at all is a capacity decision on the Hosts tab.
