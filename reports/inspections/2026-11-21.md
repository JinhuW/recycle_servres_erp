## Root-cause analysis

Nothing to fix in code. The resume loop is the code doing what it was
designed to do, and the design is what needs a decision.

- **Budget restart.** In `apps/backend/src/fleet/claim.ts:577-591` the
  heartbeat queues a resume when `fleetAutoResumeDue`
  (`packages/shared/src/fleet.ts:70`) is due. When the eight tries are spent,
  it also queues one, as try 1, if `proxyRecoveredSince`
  (`claim.ts:70-79`) finds that the worker's proxy failed an ERP check after
  the last resume and now passes a fresh one. The 00:00–00:05 outage met that
  condition for every parked worker at once. All five restarted in the same
  eleven seconds at 00:10:07–00:10:18, which is the first heartbeat after the
  00:10 round passed. Then the schedule ran 10 → 30 → 60 min.
- **The proxy is not why these workers die.** Each one parked again within
  about a minute of every resume, though resumes are only queued while the proxy passes its checks, on
  two different hosts (`homelab`, `worker-60-14`). `homelab-2`, `homelab-3`,
  `worker-60-14-4` and `worker-60-14-6` also spent their full budget last
  night. A worker whose resume always fails at once has a fault the proxy
  check can't see (the account, the session, or Facebook), so the
  proxy-recovered restart only adds logins.
- **`no_slot`** (`claim.ts`, the `no_slot` answer): there is no free fleet
  slot. Tickets minted: `worker-60-14-3` 238, `homelab-5` 236, `homelab-8`
  10, none claimed. The worker exits before it opens a browser, so these are
  not Facebook logins.

## Fixes deferred

- **Proxy-recovered restart re-arms workers whose fault isn't the proxy
  (escalating, needs the owner).** Five workers did 35 resume-logins
  tonight, and they will do 8 more the next time any proxy outage clears.
  **Decision for the owner:**
  1. Now: check the park `detail` of `homelab-2`, `homelab-3`, `homelab-6`,
     `worker-60-14-4` and `worker-60-14-6` on their worker pages. Fix or
     replace the account or proxy, or park them by hand so auto-resume stops
     (a manager park is not auto-resumed). Don't resume them by hand. Each
     resume is a login.
  2. A rule change, which is a business rule, so it was not implemented.
     Options: (a) restart the budget only if the proxy was already failing
     when the worker parked, not when any failure follows the last try;
     (b) skip the restart when the round failed most of the fleet at once,
     since that is an outage of the provider or the checker and not of this
     worker's proxy; (c) let a worker restart its budget at most once a day,
     or never if its last N resumes each died within two minutes. Each one
     is a small change to `proxyRecoveredSince` / `claim.ts:582` plus a test
     in `apps/backend/tests/fleet-claim.test.ts`.
- **Fleet capacity exceeds free slots (ninth night).** Hosts `homelab` and
  `worker-60-14` want more containers than there are free accounts.
  **Decision for the owner:** lower those hosts' capacity by the surplus on
  the Hosts tab (this stops containers and causes no login), or add accounts
  to the pool.
